About Me

I'm a passionate full-stack developer and UI/UX designer with a mission to craft clean, functional, and visually engaging digital experiences. With expertise in technologies like React, Node.js, MongoDB, and Figma, I build applications that are not only efficient but also intuitive and user-friendly. My background combines technical precision with creative design, allowing me to bridge the gap between development and visual storytelling. I take pride in writing clean, maintainable code and designing interfaces that resonate with users. I'm constantly learning and staying up-to-date with emerging technologies to ensure I deliver solutions that are modern, responsive, and impactful. Whether it's a landing page, a full-scale web app, or a mobile-first design, I approach each project with dedication, curiosity, and a user-centered mindset. My goal is to help individuals and businesses bring their ideas to life through thoughtful design and robust development.
